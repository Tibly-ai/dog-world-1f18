Just photos
of dogs, done
right.

Dog World started because our own dog, a shelter mutt named Biscuit, made every phone photo look like a bad photocopy. We were tired of dog portraits shot as an afterthought between wedding gigs and product shoots, so we made a space just for that.

It has hardwood floors dogs can actually get traction on, cheap seamless backdrops we're not afraid to get slobber on, and a treat pouch that never leaves our belt. We shoot the dog in front of us, zoomies and all, not the one on the shot list.

We've been at it in Portland since 2019, one leash tangle at a time.

The Rules of Dog World

five things we won't budge on

  1. 01

    No forced sits. If your dog wants to lie down, sniff the corner, or bark at the tripod, that's the photo. We shoot around them, never at them.

  2. 02

    Treats stay in the pocket the whole session. Every good angle we get is bribed, fairly and generously.

  3. 03

    Breaks whenever the dog needs one. We've paused sessions for water bowls, backyard laps, and one very determined nap.

  4. 04

    We don't edit out personality. Underbites, missing ears, gray muzzles and mismatched eyes stay exactly where they are.

  5. 05

    Owners are welcome in the frame. Half the best shots on the wall are of a dog leaning on the person who loves them.
